# Week 01 · Prompt 02 — Figma Make: Public Shell (Landing / Pricing / Login)

**Tool:** Figma Make (single-shot generation). **When:** Week 1, after the App Shell.

## Context

The Public shell is the Awwwards face of Griot: high-conversion marketing + auth. Different design goal from the App shell — spectacle is welcome here (GSAP/Lenis motion in Week 3), but it must *show the real product*, not describe it.

## Prompt (paste into Figma Make)

> Design the **Public marketing site** for **Griot**, a project-management web app for small teams. Dark theme that matches the product's app shell. Three pages:
>
> 1. **Landing** — hero with a *real screenshot of the Griot kanban board* (import the Week-1 App Shell board screen), a 3-feature grid (Boards, Comments & Attachments, Notifications), a social-proof row, and a CTA.
> 2. **Pricing** — 3 tiers (Free / Team / Enterprise), highlighted "Team" plan, FAQ section.
> 3. **Login** — split layout: product visual on one side, a clean email/password form on the other.
>
> Persistent sticky navigation: logo → Features → Pricing → Sign in (secondary) → Get started (primary). Trust signals at decision points. Sub-2-second-clean visual weight. Do NOT include dashboards, kanban functionality, or app-shell screens beyond the one hero screenshot.

## Done → handoff

- Refine ≥1 round, then formalize in Figma (landing components + tokens carry over from the App shell).
- Lighthouse budget recorded at deploy (Week 5); axe a11y pass required (Week 6).
